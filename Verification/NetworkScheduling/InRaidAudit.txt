已修复：整局发送及排队
1. 直连战斗消息与大包分离
   LiteNetLib 启用 2 个通道。AI_HEALTH_REPORT、AI_BUFF_REPORT、PLAYER_DAMAGE_REQUEST、
   PLAYER_DAMAGE_FORWARD、PLAYER_BUFF_SELF_APPLY 的可靠有序消息走通道 1；
   带时间戳的 AI/玩家血量广播继续可靠无序，也走通道 1。
   元数据、完整/轻量快照、Buff 广播、物品事务、场景消息和玩家死亡报告保留通道 0。
   玩家死亡报告必须先于尸体库存到达，不能一并移动到战斗通道。
   两个通道不保证彼此之间的顺序；AI 血量结果依靠已有时间戳和缺失模型缓存处理。
   在接收侧设置独立队列，每连续最多 3 条战斗消息后给普通队列 1 次处理机会；
   保持每个队列内顺序、可靠事件不合并、4ms/512 次每帧预算，以及原位置样本合并逻辑。
   该通道优化针对 Direct/局域网；没有改动 Steam 的传输通道实现。

2. 周期敌人刷新不再重复发送模型、外观和装备
   每个连接独立记录实际发送过的元数据签名。首次同步、元数据变化、强制恢复仍发完整快照。
   签名排除位置、速度、血量、护甲、Buff、激活状态和当前持有武器等动态字段。
   元数据相同时改发 AI_REFRESH_CHUNK：只含动态状态、血量时间戳、元数据版本和范围判断标志。
   接收端保留已有模型/装备字典，继续按原有规则应用血量、Buff、位置和武器状态。
   客机正常周期请求时，主机抑制额外周期推送；客机不再刷新时仍保留主机兜底。
   车辆每 6 秒的请求不会错误延长普通敌人的刷新期限。
   主机兜底调度只排 ID，不再先构建完整快照、到实际发送时又构建一遍。

3. 保留恢复能力，避免轻量快照永久沿用旧装备
   完整快照和出生消息在可选尾部携带元数据版本；轻量快照携带匹配版本。
   客机若因范围/队列预算跳过完整更新，版本不匹配会触发现有受限频的 ForceFull 恢复。
   元数据缺失时不从轻量包直接创建敌人。延后应用的旧完整快照不能覆盖较新的出生元数据。
   轻量包仍携带车辆/原生区域的范围标志，故意剔除的远处对象不会反复触发全量恢复。
   切图/重置清空服务端与客户端基线；断线清空该玩家的服务端缓存；ForceFull 清空该连接基线。
   完整与轻量状态继续共用通道 0，保留和 Buff 快照/广播之间的接收顺序。

Receive-queue robustness: 1.4.2.7
- Direct receive diagnostics report every 5 seconds with activity: outstanding counts/bytes by opcode, interval arrivals/handled counts, total/max handler time, max handled wait, global oldest pending age and native PollEvents time. Summary selects the top four handler costs and top four pending counts. Coalesced/disconnected/stopped buffers are released from accounting; transport generations protect a Stop invoked inside a handler.
- Age starts when the native callback queues the payload, not when it reached the socket or was sent. Handler time does not include later async asset work. AI ClientQueue logs separately measure first accepted metadata to actor activation (receiveToVisible), build duration and spawn/recovery queue counts; activation does not guarantee a rendered frame.
- Direct clients send coalesced unreliable pressure feedback once/second. Server lowers only that peer's periodic AI pose rate beyond 40m and periodic snapshot rate at >=256 pending or >=150ms age; >=1024 or >=500ms selects stronger reduction. Critical/direct events, vehicles, near poses, initial full snapshots and targeted repairs bypass advisory throttling. Reliable send-queue gating still applies. Three healthy samples restore quality; absent feedback expires after 5s. Steam reports unsupported and keeps existing scheduling.
- Periodic poses resend at least once per second when eligible, including idle actors, so skipped final positions eventually converge. This heartbeat slightly increases idle traffic compared with permanent signature suppression.
- Unknown AI health/state/Buff/vehicle data and missing compact metadata request only the affected entity. Requests carry main-scene identity, use per-entity 2s cooldown and per-peer token buckets (8 burst, 4/s refill), and queue at most 32 IDs. Responses force full metadata for that peer without resetting the rest of the scene; host samples live state when sending. Existing periodic refresh provides another retry opportunity. Snapshot overflow requests incremental recovery, not another forced full-scene burst.
- This is not a model-ready acknowledgement protocol or a full scene/lifecycle epoch retrofit. The player position/animation fan-out and Unity asset cost remain separate limits; reliable event buffers are not hard-capped or silently discarded.
- Release build passed (0 errors, 25 existing warnings); 24 scheduling/wire checks passed, including per-peer isolation, pressure hysteresis/expiry, request burst limits, legacy request tails and native retained-buffer feedback.
